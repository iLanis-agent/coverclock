CoverClock

Every receipt is a countdown you can't see. The return window closes, the warranty lapses, and you find out the day after. CoverClock puts every purchase on a visible timer.

Track a purchase

Add an item with its purchase date, warranty length, and return window. The dashboard sorts everything by urgency: what needs action this week floats to the top, with live countdowns, progress bars, and the total value still under warranty. Everything stays in your browser.
